# coffee-cart — schema

Extra rules for this domain, on top of the vault's [[AGENTS]].

## Scope
What this domain covers: deciding whether to run a weekend coffee cart, where, at what price, with which equipment and permits.
What it does NOT cover: personal finances (those stay in personal-admin, which the agent never opens).

## Page types
- `wiki/sources/`: one summary per raw source
- `wiki/`: concept, person, organisation and decision pages

## Domain-specific rules
- Every money figure states the currency, the date it was quoted, and its source.
- Foot-traffic numbers always say *when* they were counted (day and time).
- A decision page lists the options considered, the reasons, and what would make us reverse it.
