Notes from coffee with Sam (runs Corner Café, 2 streets from the market square) — 2 Sept 2026

- Has run the café 6 years. Busiest: Saturday 8–12 when the market is on. "Half my Saturday is market people."
- Flat white is €3.20, filter €2.40. Raised prices last spring, "nobody blinked".
- Thinks a cart at the market square would hurt him a bit on Saturdays but says the square is "big enough for two". Friendly, offered to share his milk supplier.
- Weekday mornings at the square are dead, he says. Commuters go to the station.
- Warned me: council takes ages with licences. Took him 2 months for his terrace permit.
- Beans: he roasts nothing himself, buys from a roaster ~€20/kg (didn't say which).
