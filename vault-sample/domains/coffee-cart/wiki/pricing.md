# Pricing

**In one sentence:** matching the local café's prices leaves a healthy margin per cup; beans are a small part of the cost.

## Key points
- Local reference: flat white €3.20, filter €2.40 at Corner Café, Sept 2026 ([[source-interview-sam]]).
- Planned price: flat white **€3.00**, slightly under the café, to win strollers. *(See the lint report: a session note says €3.20.)*
- Beans: €18–21/kg ([[source-supplier-quotes]]). At ~18 g per double shot, that's about **€0.32–0.38 of coffee per drink** (calculated, not quoted).

## Details
Milk, cups and lids are not costed yet. Ask Sam for his milk supplier's prices (he offered, [[source-interview-sam]]).

## Open questions
- Full cost per cup including milk and a cup.
- Will market visitors pay the same as café customers?
