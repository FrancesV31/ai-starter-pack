# Permits

**In one sentence:** a council street trading licence costs €420/year and takes 6–8 weeks, but only covers council land.

## Key points
- €420/year, fixed pitch, 6–8 weeks after a complete application ([[source-permit-office-call]]).
- Requirements: level 2 food hygiene certificate, public liability insurance, a photo of the cart ([[source-permit-office-call]]).
- Sam's terrace permit took about 2 months, consistent with the official estimate ([[source-interview-sam]]).
- Does **not** apply to the station forecourt (rail company land) ([[source-permit-office-call]]).

## Checklist before applying
- [ ] Food hygiene certificate
- [ ] Insurance
- [ ] Cart bought (photo needed)
- [ ] Confirm the call's details in writing
