# Decision: start at the market square on Saturdays

**In one sentence:** apply now for a Saturday pitch at the market square, and ask the rail company about the station in parallel.

**Decided:** 2026-10-05, in session [[2026-10-05-go-no-go-prep]].

## Options considered
1. **Station exit, weekday mornings.** Most people, but they rarely stop, and the land owner's process is unknown.
2. **Market square, Saturdays.** Fewer hours, but people who stroll and stop, a known licence process, and pitches free now.
3. **Wait** until both are clear. Loses the autumn season.

## Why option 2
- Saturday traffic at the square (480–560/hour) is close to the station's, with people who stop ([[source-foot-traffic-count]]).
- The council process is known: €420, 6–8 weeks, 2 pitches free ([[source-permit-office-call]]).
- One day a week tests the idea with low risk before committing to weekday mornings.

## What would make us reverse it
- The rail company offers a forecourt pitch at a similar price.
- Saturday sales below 60 drinks per morning for 4 weeks in a row.
