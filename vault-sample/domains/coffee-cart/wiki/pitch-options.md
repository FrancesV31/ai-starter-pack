# Pitch options

**In one sentence:** the station has more people on weekday mornings, but the market square has people who *stop*, and it's the only pitch we can get a licence for quickly.

## Key points
- **Station exit:** 390–430 people/hour on a Tuesday morning ([[source-foot-traffic-count]]), but walkers rarely stop.
- **Market square:** quiet on weekdays (160–200/hour) but 480–560/hour on Saturday mornings ([[source-foot-traffic-count]]), and people stroll.
- Sam confirms Saturday is market-driven and weekdays are dead at the square ([[source-interview-sam]]).
- The station forecourt is **rail company land**: a council licence doesn't cover it ([[source-permit-office-call]]).
- 2 Saturday pitches are free at the square right now ([[source-permit-office-call]]).

## Comparison

| | Station exit | Market square |
|---|---|---|
| Morning foot traffic | High on weekdays | High on Saturdays only |
| People who stop | Few | Many |
| Who grants the pitch | Rail company (unknown process) | Council (€420/year, 6–8 weeks) |
| Competition | None seen | [[sam-corner-cafe]] two streets away |
