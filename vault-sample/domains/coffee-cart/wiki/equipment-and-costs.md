# Equipment and start-up costs

**In one sentence:** a used cart keeps the start-up budget around €7,000; a new one roughly doubles it.

## Key points
- Used cart with a lever espresso machine on gas: €6,500, needs new tyres, not inspected ([[source-supplier-quotes]]).
- New cart: €11,000 plus about €3,000 for a machine ([[source-supplier-quotes]]).
- Hilltop lends a grinder for 3 months, which delays one purchase ([[source-supplier-quotes]]).
- Licence: €420/year ([[source-permit-office-call]]).
