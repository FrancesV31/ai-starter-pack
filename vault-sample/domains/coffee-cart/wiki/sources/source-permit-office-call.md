# Source: Call with the permit office

**What it is:** my notes from a 15-minute phone call.

## Key points
- Licence €420/year, fixed pitch, 6–8 weeks to process.
- Requires a level 2 food hygiene certificate, liability insurance, a photo of the cart.
- 2 Saturday pitches are free at the market square.
- The station forecourt belongs to the rail company: the council licence doesn't apply there.
