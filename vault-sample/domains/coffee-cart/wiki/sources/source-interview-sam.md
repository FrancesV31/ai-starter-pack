# Source: Interview with Sam (Corner Café)

**What it is:** my notes from an informal 30-minute chat.

## Key points
- Saturday 8–12 is his busiest time, driven by the market.
- Prices: flat white €3.20, filter €2.40 (Sept 2026).
- Weekday mornings at the square are quiet; commuters use the station.
- Council licences are slow (2 months for his terrace permit).
- Buys beans at about €20/kg.

## Notable quotes
> "Half my Saturday is market people."
