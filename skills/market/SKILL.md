# Market — fast competitive scan

Research the market for the idea or product space the user gives. If the user didn't say what it's about, ask them first.

Use web search for current information. Then give:
- **Landscape:** the main players and alternatives, including the "do nothing / status quo" option, one line each.
- **Positioning gaps:** white space or weak incumbents.
- **Differentiation angles:** how a new entrant could realistically win.
- **Market signals:** demand, trends, timing (cite sources with links).
- **Risks:** saturation, moats, regulation.

Be honest if the space looks crowded or the idea looks weak. Clearly flag anything you could not verify.
